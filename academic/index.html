Marsed Leti, PhD

Lecturer · Department of Civil Engineering, Epoka University
mleti@epoka.edu.al Rinas, km 12, Tiranë, Albania

Civil engineer and academic working at the intersection of structural engineering and computation. My teaching and research centre on the seismic behaviour of reinforced concrete structures — how existing residential building stock actually performs under strong ground motion, and how non-linear analysis can make that performance measurable rather than assumed.

Over thirteen years of teaching I have taken roughly ten courses a year across the undergraduate and graduate programmes, ranging from engineering drawing and programming fundamentals to structural analysis and computer applications in civil engineering, alongside supervision of master theses and independent research. I also serve on scientific committees for three conferences and take part in the activities of the EAAA association.

Research focus

  • Seismic performance assessment of reinforced concrete template buildings, with emphasis on the residential stock built to repeated standard designs.
  • Non-linear analysis procedures — pushover and non-linear time history analysis, and the modelling decisions that drive their results.
  • Incremental Dynamic Analysis (IDA) and fragility-oriented evaluation of building response across ground motion intensities.
  • Computational tools in structural engineering — scripting, automation, and teaching engineers to build their own analysis workflows.

Academic experience

  • Lecturer, Department of Civil Engineering, Epoka University — undergraduate and graduate teaching, thesis supervision, and course coordination. Around 10 courses delivered per academic year.
  • Master thesis supervision (CE 500) and supervised independent study and research (CE 507) for graduate students.
  • Scientific committee member for three conferences.
  • EAAA — member and contributor to the association's activities.
